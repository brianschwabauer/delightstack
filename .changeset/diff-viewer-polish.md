DiffViewer polish: line numbers and text align on the baseline, so a number no longer floats above the line it labels. Changed words are small tinted chips: an insertion is a plain tint with no underline, a deletion keeps its strike in muted text, and a hairline gap keeps an adjacent deletion and insertion from reading as one word. The bar that expands hidden lines shows an unfold glyph.
